Export Cleaner

This app needs desktop Chrome.

It opens a folder on your computer with the File System Access API, which Safari, Firefox and mobile browsers don't support.

Export Cleaner

Open an export folder, sort each family of files into keep or reject, then clean up.

Pick the folder your exports go into. Chrome won't open Downloads, Desktop or Documents themselves, only folders inside them.

Your files never leave your computer.
